About

A bit about me

I'm Abigail, a Lagos-based Virtual Assistant and CRM Specialist with a background spanning administration, sales, and customer-facing roles. After studying English and International Studies, I built hands-on experience in pharmacy supervision, teaching, and admin coordination — before training intensively in CRM systems and remote support tools to focus on what I do best: keeping things running smoothly behind the scenes.

Today, I support businesses with CRM setup and management, workflow automation, and day-to-day virtual assistance. I also serve as Secretary at St. Peter's Anglican Church, Ikota, where I manage correspondence, documentation, and coordination for the parish — work that sharpened the same skills I bring to every client: precision, follow-through, and calm under deadlines.
